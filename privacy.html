Summary

In plain words: this website has no accounts, sign-up forms or comment boxes, so it does not directly collect personal information. Like almost every website, the companies that host it and show ads on it may process technical data such as your IP address. We do not sell personal information collected through this website.

Scope of this policy

This policy explains how the Zunx Client website (the pages you are reading now) handles information. The Zunx Client launcher is a separate program. Where the launcher shows its own notices or connects to services such as Microsoft sign-in, those notices and the policies of those services also apply.

Information we collect

Zunx Client does not directly collect personal information through this website. Although some launcher features may require an account to be created, that is separate from browsing this site.

  • Technical data from our hosts. Our hosting providers (such as GitHub and Netlify) may collect standard technical information, for example IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and pages visited, as part of normal website operation and security.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us or message us on a social platform, we receive the details you choose to include, such as your email address and the content of your message. We use them only to reply and to improve Zunx.

Cookies and local storage

We do not set our own tracking cookies. The website stores one small preference in your browser's local storage: whether you chose the light or dark theme. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising partners (see below) may set cookies or use similar technologies in your browser. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings, although blocking them may change which ads you see.

Advertising

We use third-party advertising services, including Adsterra, to display advertisements on our website. They and their partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, measure and improve ads, limit how often you see an ad, and show ads that may be relevant to you.

For more information, see Adsterra's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services. You can manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com, and many ad networks offer opt-outs through youradchoices.com or youronlinechoices.eu.

Ads are provided by third parties. We do not control their content or their data practices, and we ask you to take care before clicking or downloading anything from an advertisement.

Third-party services and links

  • GitHub. Installers are hosted on GitHub Releases, and the download page asks GitHub for the list of releases. GitHub's privacy policy applies when you access or download files from GitHub.
  • Microsoft, Xbox and Mojang. If you sign in with a Microsoft account in the launcher, sign-in is handled by those services under their own privacy policies.
  • Discord, YouTube and Facebook. The footer links to our pages on these platforms. Once you open them, the platform's own terms and privacy policy apply, and they may collect data about your visit.
  • Mod, shader and skin sources. Features that search for or download content contact the websites that host that content.

The launcher and your Minecraft account

You choose how to play: offline with a name, or with a Microsoft account. Features such as skin sharing between Zunx players need the skin you pick to be visible to other Zunx players. Never share your Microsoft password with anyone. No one from Zunx will ask for it.

How we use information

  • To run, secure and improve the website.
  • To reply to messages and give support.
  • To keep the launcher free through advertising shown by third parties.

We do not sell personal information collected through this website, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles ourselves.

How long data is kept

We do not keep a database of website visitors. Technical logs held by hosting and advertising providers are kept under their own retention rules. Emails and messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you and to keep a record of support requests.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS by our hosting providers. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Download the launcher only from this website or the official GitHub releases page.

Children

Minecraft is popular with young players, but this website is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, please ask a parent or guardian before contacting us or using sign-in services. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information about you, to object to certain processing, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising. Because we do not keep a database of visitors, most of this data sits with the hosting and advertising providers named above, and you can use their tools. To ask us about anything we hold, email the address below.

You can also block cookies, use your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker, and clear local storage at any time.

International visitors

Our hosting and advertising providers operate in several countries, so technical data may be processed outside the country where you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, the launcher or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Continuing to use the website after an update means you accept the updated policy.

Privacy FAQ

Do you track me?

We do not run our own analytics or tracking on this website. Hosting providers and advertising partners may process technical data and set cookies, as described above.

Do you sell my data?

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. Third-party ad networks run their own programs, which are covered by their own policies.

Can I use the website without ads or cookies?

You can block cookies and use an ad blocker. The website still works, although some ad-related features may behave differently.

What is stored on my device?

Only your light or dark theme choice, in your browser's local storage. Advertising partners may store cookies of their own.

Does the website need my Microsoft login?

No. The website has no login. Microsoft sign-in only appears inside the launcher, and it is optional because you can also play offline.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Email us at the address in the Contact section. For data held by GitHub, Netlify or ad networks, use those companies' own privacy tools.

Is the launcher covered by this policy?

This policy covers the website. The launcher is a separate program that shows its own notices where needed, and services it connects to apply their own policies.
