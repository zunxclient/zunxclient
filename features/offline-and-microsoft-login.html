Offline or Microsoft login

You choose how to play. Use your real Microsoft account, or play offline with any name you like.

How it helps you

  • Sign in with Microsoft to join online servers with your own account.
  • Prefer to just play? Use offline mode and pick a name (3 to 16 letters or numbers).
  • Keep more than one account and switch between them.
  • You are always in control of which account is used.

How to use it

  1. Open Settings or the account area in Zunx.
  2. Choose Microsoft sign-in, or enter an offline name.
  3. Pick the account and press Play.

Note: offline accounts cannot change their skin on Mojang servers. Zunx still lets other Zunx players see your chosen skin.
