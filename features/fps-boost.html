One-click FPS boost

FPS means frames per second. A higher number makes the game feel smoother. Zunx can install a set of well known performance mods for you.

How it helps you

  • Install a ready made list of speed mods with one click.
  • The list includes well known mods such as Sodium, Lithium and FerriteCore (the exact list depends on your loader).
  • Great for older computers and laptops.
  • No need to search for each mod by yourself.
  • Prefer no mods at all? OptiFine can also raise FPS without mods.

How to use it

  1. Open your instance.
  2. Press the FPS boost button.
  3. Wait a moment, then press Play.
